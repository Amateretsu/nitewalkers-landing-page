Plex

Offline

Movies and TV shows, streamed from our server to whatever screen you're on.

What you need

  • The Plex app (phone, TV, or plex.tv in a browser)
  • A free Plex account

Setup steps

1

Download the Plex app from the App Store, Google Play, or a smart TV app store — or just go to plex.tv on a computer.

2

Sign up for a free Plex account, or sign in if you already have one.

3

Ask us to add you to the server. We'll send an invite to the email on your Plex account.

4

Accept the invite, open the "Nite Owls" library in the app, and start watching.

Stuck? Just ask — happy to help you get it working.
