Vintage Story

Offline

A slower-paced, password-protected survival world, up whenever you want to play.

What you need

  • Vintage Story, installed and up to date
  • The server address and password — ask us for them

Setup steps

1

Launch Vintage Story and choose "Multiplayer" from the main menu.

2

Click "Add Server" and enter the address we give you.

3

Select the server and connect, entering the password when prompted.

Don't have the address or password? Just ask.
